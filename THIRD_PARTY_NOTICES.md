# Third-party references, artwork, and runtime packages

The portfolio owner supplied four ZIP references. Their instructions were treated as reference material, not as product requirements. The implementation adapts their interaction principles to Daniyal's content, native page scroll, accessibility model, and carbon/paper art direction.

## Supplied interaction references

- **Rotating On-Scroll Animations** — Codrops, MIT License, Copyright 2009–2025 Codrops. Its scroll-linked 3D image rotation informed the repository atlas. The demo's stock photographs are not shipped; original project-specific visual plates replace them.
- **Infinite Canvas** — Edoardo Lunardi / Codrops, MIT License, Copyright 2009–2025 Codrops. Its spatial media-field principle informed the native-scroll evidence canvas. Eighteen artwork files supplied with the demo are retained as requested; the demo credits them to [The Art Institute of Chicago Open Access](https://www.artic.edu/open-access/open-access-images).
- **On-Scroll Text Motion** — Codrops, MIT License, Copyright 2009–2025 Codrops. Its distributed-word choreography informed the three operating-method scenes. All words, layout, and motion timing are Daniyal-specific.
- **Draggable Infinite Slider** — resource credit in the supplied source: [Osmo](https://www.osmo.supply/). The reference was evaluated and initially adapted, then removed from the production homepage because it repeated the repository-atlas content and kept an offscreen marquee running. No InertiaPlugin dependency or demo imagery is shipped.

## Runtime dependencies

The implementation uses the packages declared in `package.json`, including Next.js, React, Geist, GSAP, Lenis, Three.js, React Three Fiber, Drei, and `@14islands/r3f-scroll-rig`, under their respective upstream licenses and terms.

- GSAP owns the authored intro and bounded scroll-linked timelines.
- Lenis is the homepage's single smooth-scroll owner.
- Next.js and React provide the application runtime.
- Geist provides the interface typeface.
- Three.js remains available to the bounded SilkWaves case-study proof and the archived, currently unmounted universe experiment.
