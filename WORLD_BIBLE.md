# Editorial Motion World Bible

World sentence: move from a black liquid-metal identity into a precise paper/carbon evidence system, where every visual shift reflects a change in confidence rather than a change of decorative theme.

Interaction contract: native document structure owns meaning; Lenis refines scroll on full-motion sessions; pointer, touch, and keyboard provide bounded local response without hiding content or changing the route.

## Constants

- Spatial model: editorial chapters, sticky project plates, full-bleed material interstitial, and fixed chapter wayfinding.
- Shape grammar: full-bleed material, hairline frames, circles for state/waypoints, compact operating labels, and deliberately cropped proof surfaces.
- Palette: carbon `#08090b`, paper `#f0f0ea`, ink `#090a0d`, silver, white, and signal cobalt `#405ae4`.
- Type grammar: oversized grotesk display, neutral reading copy, uppercase micro labels, and tabular operating numbers.
- Media rule: owner-supplied liquid-metal film appears only in the opening identity and one derived material transition.
- 3D rule: no Three.js, WebGL, canvas renderer, shader, procedural 3D, astronaut, or spatial camera.

## Performance budget

- Preserve one Lenis owner and one GSAP/ScrollTrigger motion owner.
- Keep page JS near the current 65.6KB route / 168KB first-load baseline unless a new feature proves its value.
- Hero poster remains high-priority; hero film pauses offscreen, hidden, reduced-motion, or save-data sessions.
- No offscreen CSS loops, duplicate smooth-scroll engines, continuous idle cursor RAF, or unbounded timers.
- Use transform/opacity/clip-path intentionally; limit blur to short entrance windows.

## Lifecycle

Pause media and motion on `document.hidden`; resume from the same scroll state; refresh ScrollTrigger after font/media readiness; cancel timers and RAF; remove listeners; disconnect observers; destroy Lenis; revert GSAP contexts; and keep semantic content usable without JavaScript.
