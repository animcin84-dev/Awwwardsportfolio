## Motion grammar

- GSAP owns composed and scroll-linked motion; Lenis is the only smooth-scroll engine.
- `power4.out`, `expo.out`, and the shared authored easing tokens establish the motion family.
- Hero pointer response stays below 8px; other parallax remains shallow and content never depends on it.
- Titles and media remain understandable when static; links and controls are never split into decorative motion fragments.
- Reduced motion removes Lenis and scrubs, renders final states immediately, and serves the poster without a video source.
- Hidden documents pause Lenis/ticker and hero media; every observer, listener, timer, RAF, and GSAP context has cleanup.

## Performance envelope

- No Three.js, WebGL, canvas, physics, or continuously rendered background.
- One 75KB poster is the complete first frame; the approximately 3MB hero film is progressive and may move to a CDN.
- Below-the-fold imagery is DOM/CSS-authored; there are no decorative image grids or autoplay videos.
- Current production route: 65.6KB page JS and 168KB first-load JS before launch hosting optimizations.
